# Solo FFA vs nations/tribes — one order per 50-tick decision

## Ethos
- Survival is the score; peak tiles mean nothing if you lose them. maxTroops = 2*(tiles^0.6*1000+50000) + 250k per city level (Medium nation x0.75, tribe /3). Tiles raise the ceiling, cities raise it far more; density (troops/tiles) is your defence, and a thin sprawl is bait.
- Every decision do one thing: expand, raid a spent/collapsed neighbour, or build/upgrade. No-ops waste refill and tempo. Cities are the growth engine — build and upgrade them early and often; a handful of cities in a long run is a losing number. Income is 100 gold/tick plus port/factory trade; gold only counts when spent.
- Grow by eating the weak, never by trading with the strong. Medium nations pick off the weakest bordering enemy, any Hostile relation it beats by up to 3x, and AFK/traitor neighbours; a stronger neighbour will keep selecting you. Stay above every bordering non-ally's army.
- Attacking forecloses diplomacy: -70 relation, auto-embargo, and it rejects its pending alliance. Choose ally-or-attack once. Breaking an alliance gives 300 ticks at 0.5x defence / 0.8x speed and near-universal refusal — don't.
- Taking land under enemy structures captures them (defense posts are destroyed). Raids into a structure-rich nation are the fastest way to raise your own max.
- Commit one decisive front: splitting your army across two strong nations in consecutive decisions leaves home at reserve and invites conquest.

## When to act
- RIVAL CADENCE: Medium nations act every 55-70 ticks (tribes 40-80), only at >=reserve (30-40% of max) and 90% of the time at >=trigger (50-60%; 10% act below). Refill/tick = (10 + troops^0.73/4)*(1 - troops/max)*0.95. Troops climbing on flat tiles = still refilling; pinned near max = an act is imminent. Nations spend their act on bordering tribes first, so a nation ringed by tribes may ignore you.
- COUNTER WINDOW: after its wave lands its home is short by that wave; strike the same decision you see the drop, before its next act 55-70 ticks later. Draining it below reserve buys quiet ticks (it will not act). Attacking it cancels its incoming attack troop-for-troop — send strictly more than its incoming to cancel AND press.
- FORCED RETALIATION: if one incoming_attacks count >= ~half your troops, or is the largest and its attacker borders_human, spend the decision on it: send >= that count at the attacker. Land cannot reach a non-bordering attacker — boat one of its boat_targets or request alliance (an accepted alliance makes the ongoing attack retreat). Unanswered waves stack and kill you.
- ALLIANCES (Medium accepts if): your troops > theirs*2.5 (threat overrides even Hostile); OR relation Friendly; OR tick < ~1900 (70%); OR similarly strong (their troops+outgoing > ~70-80% of yours, or their tiles > ~80-90% with troops > half of yours). Refused if relation < Neutral, if you are a traitor, or if they already hold ~4-5. Tribes always accept and extend. Ally bordering non-hostile nations and tribes in the early window as shields; renew before the 3000-tick lapse (only you can request it). An ally betrays once it out-troops you ~10x, or you fall under 20% of max.
- DEFENSE POSTS (land only): a Medium nation gets 1 post, 50% chance per structure call, once LAND incoming (boat excluded) reaches 35% of its troops; calls land on its act tick and at 1/3 and 2/3 through. Keep each land alpha under 35% of its troops, or hit >30 tiles from its existing post, or come by boat. A post multiplies your per-tile loss x5 and its speed x3 within range 30: never grind one with land.
- DOGPILE: a target with incoming_troops > 0.5*its troops is the engine's victim; if its troops <= 1.2x yours it is collapsing — race a bordering one to its land (under 100 tiles it is auto-conquered and its land splits among neighbours). Conversely your own incoming total > 0.5*your troops with an attacker within 1.2x marks YOU as prey: stop attacking, ally or retreat.

## Attack sizing (D = target troops, T = target tiles; never a fixed share)
- Per-tile loss vs a player: L = 0.6*clamp(D/A,0.6,2)*m*0.8*f(T) + 0.4*1.3*(D/T)*(m/100); f(T) ~1 under 30k tiles, ~0.85 at 150k. m = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, x0.7 vs a tribe (Bot), x5 if the defender has a post in range 30. Terrain isn't exposed: plan on plains and pad 25-50%.
- Clear cost ~= L*T, plus defender regen while you crawl. An attack that runs out of troops is deleted, not refunded — you lose the whole committed force. Launch only if cost <= troops - reserve (reserve >= strongest bordering non-ally and >= ~35% of your max). A ~ D is the knee; use A in [D, 1.7D]. Below D/2 the 2x clamp roughly doubles per-tile loss; past 1.7D you only reach the 0.6 floor. A target with D > ~1.7x your A is not clearable — hit it only to cancel a wave.
- Neutral land: cost = (m/5)*T (16 / 20 / 24 per plains / highland / mountain tile), no defender loss. Send ~= cost and spread spare troops across frontiers rather than one.
- Worked: neutral 1,000 plains -> 16k clears; 3,000 highland -> 60k. Tribe D=60k / T=1,500 plains: L ~30-38 -> ~50-58k clears (send ~D). Nation D=200k / T=3,000 plains: L ~55-66 -> ~165-200k clears; its post pushes L ~180 (~550k) — boat or skip. Never send sub-scale trickles (e.g. 130-150k into a 1M-troop neighbour): they only mark you Hostile, feed retaliation, and buy no land.
- BOATS: 3 max, 1 tile/tick, free. A landing ignores the land-post trigger and cancels the target's incoming troop-for-troop; size it like a land strike since it attacks from the shore. Keep all three sailing at reachable weak/neutral boat_targets; recall costs 25%. Expansion must never stall against water — with no land border, boat.
