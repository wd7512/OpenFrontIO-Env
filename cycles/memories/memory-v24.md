# Solo FFA playbook (Medium nations + many tribes): act every decision, size from the board

## Ethos
- One order per 50-tick decision; a no-op is a lost decision. Every turn, do something: expand into neutral, shove a stalled front, or build/upgrade a city. A front's survivors return free when it closes.
- Growth is tiles + cities: maxTroops = 2*(tiles^0.6*1000+50000) + 250k per city level (a Medium nation's cap is x0.75 of yours, a tribe's is x1/3, so you out-scale at equal land). Conquered cities transfer to you (+250k each); defense posts are destroyed, not taken. Your gold is 100/tick and own cities cost 125k then double.
- One war at a time; finish it or don't start. Cancelling/retreating vs a player burns 25% of the wave (free vs neutral). Attacking a nation is -70 relation + auto-embargo on Medium, so it is a permanent enemy: only start what one or two waves can kill.
- Never land-attack a player you don't border: troops come back, but you eat the -70 and the embargo. Use a boat or skip.
- Survival gates everything: keep home troops above the strongest bordering non-allied rival while any wave is out. Never idle while Hostile — a nation you half-killed will counter and take everything.

## When to act
- CADENCE: a Medium nation acts every 55-70 ticks, a tribe every 40-80; both need 50-60% of maxTroops to attack and hold a 30-40% reserve. Reading every 50 ticks, a rival that just acted sits near reserve with tiles flat; it refills ~5-8% of max per decision and skips act ticks below trigger, so it is passive ~2-4 decisions. That is the counter window — hit then.
- TARGET PRIORITY: on its act tick a nation first expands into neutral and returns; a Medium nation attacks a bordering tribe/bot before it retaliates. A nation with neutral or tribe borders often won't counter you that tick; one without them throws everything above its reserve at its largest non-bot incoming attacker on its next act. Size your home for that.
- FORCED RETALIATION: same-target land attacks merge, and a head-on attack cancels an incoming attack troop-for-troop at launch. If incoming_attacks show one hit >= ~50% of your troops, send at least that much at its attacker (if bordering); the largest incoming attacker is the rival's chosen counter-target too.
- ALLIANCES: threat overrides relation. A Medium nation accepts when your troops > 2.5x its (even Hostile), or it is Friendly, or similarly strong (your total troops > 70-80% of its, or your tiles > 80-90% of its with troops > 0.5x), or early (<~tick 1900). Tribes accept every request. Ask early, renew before the 3000-tick expiry, keep ~4-6 allies. A traitor is refused for 300 ticks, and an attacked target is Hostile — decide the alliance before you attack.
- DEFENSE POSTS: land attacks only (sourceTile null). Once the sum of a rival's incoming land troops is >= 35% of its troops, Medium has a 50% chance per structure call (~every 1/3 act interval) to raise one, max 1. Keep simultaneous land pressure on one nation under 35% of its current troops, land one big first wave, or open a boat front: a landing (sourceTile set) never counts toward the 35%. A post within 30 tiles multiplies your per-tile loss x5 and cuts capture speed x3 — attack a shore >30 tiles away. Build your own when your incoming land troops reach 35% of your troops (first post 50k).
- DOGPILE: incoming_troops >= 50% of a rival's own troops (the engine's victim test) with rival troops <= ~1.2x yours means it is committed and counters weakly — hit it before an idle equal. At/above its troop count it is collapsing: race to share a border, because a dead player's land is split among adjacent players.
- BOATS: 3 free, 1 tile/tick; boat_targets only reach straight cardinal water rays (owner/troops/tiles given). Landings bypass the post trigger and walls and still cancel the target's incoming attack; a boat reaching home or cancelled loses 25%. Use them for water-stalled fronts, islands and post-backed shores, and order them ahead when the land line bogs down.

## Attack sizing (derive from D, T, density, terrain — never a fixed share)
- Per-tile attacker loss vs a player: L = 0.48*clamp(D/A,0.6,2)*m + 0.52*(D/T)*(m/100), where m = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, x0.7 if the target is a tribe, x5 if a defense post covers the tile. D = target troops, A = troops sent, T = target tiles. A defender over ~150k tiles shaves term 1 by up to 30%; you gain term-1 and travel bonuses once you hold >100k tiles.
- A = D is the knee: it reaches the floor per-tile travel cost (D/(5A) at 0.2) and maxes border expansion. 1.0-1.7D is the efficient band; below ~1D you pay up to 1.5x travel cost and cannot outrun regen, above ~1.7D you strip home for no gain. The defender loses D/T per tile taken, so clearing all T tiles removes ~D: multiply L by T and only go if the whole cost fits the wave.
- Regen while a front is open ~ (10 + D^0.73/4)*(1 - D/maxD) per tick (Medium nation x0.95, tribe x0.5) — often tens of thousands per decision. If you cannot finish in 1-2 decisions, don't start.
- Neutral land ("expand"): loss is flat m/5 = 16/20/24 per tile regardless of A. Any A >= ~6600 plains (8000 highland, 10000 mountain) reaches the minimum travel cost and moves ~0.4 x frontier tiles/tick; send more only across extra frontiers. Cost ~ per-tile x tiles.
- Worked examples:
  - Tribe, 800 tiles / 50k plains (m 56): D/T = 62. A = 1.5D = 75k -> clamp 0.67, L = 18 + 18 ~ 36/tile, full clear ~29k. An A = 15k probe -> clamp 2, L ~ 72/tile, dies.
  - Nation, 3,000 tiles / 200k plains (m 80): D/T = 67. A = 1.4D = 280k -> clamp 0.71, L ~ 27 + 28 = 55/tile, full clear ~165k -> one to two decisions. With a post L ~ 275/tile (~825k): boat a clear shore or skip.
  - Neutral, 1,000 plains: ~16/tile -> ~16k total; send ~max(6600, frontier width) and keep the rest home.
- A target that survives a wave regens and stays Hostile: resize, boat it, or switch — never repeat the same oversized wave.
