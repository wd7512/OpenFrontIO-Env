# OpenFront solo playbook — tempo, water, exchange rates

## Ethos
- One decision = 50 ticks (5 s). Issue something every decision: a sized attack, a boat, a build, or a counter. Never idle twice. After each order check the next snapshot's `attacks`/`units`: if it never appeared it was blocked (spawn immunity, no shared border, friendly/self target) — re-aim, don't resend the same order.
- You stall only against water or a defended front. Keep a boat moving whenever `boat_targets` is non-empty and a land push on the weakest bordering region; if `tiles` hasn't risen in two decisions you are stalling — change the axis, not just the size.
- growth/tick = (10 + troops^0.73/4)·(1 − troops/max); max = 2·(tiles^0.6·1000 + 50000) + 250k·completed city levels. Human keeps full max and full growth; Medium nation max ×0.75, growth ×0.95; tribe max ×1/3, growth ×0.5. Equal land you out-refill any nation, so the only question is whether the push finishes.
- Attacking spends troops and raises refill ((1−troops/max)↑), so never bank troops — convert them into finishable tiles — and never bank gold: first city 125k (+250k cap), then 250k/500k/1M. A target you stop halfway refills against you; either take it or don't open it.
- A nation places a structure ~3× per cadence and each city lifts its max by 250k; a rival buying cities is becoming unkillable — finish it or ally it before its ceiling outruns you.

## When to act
- Rival cadence: Medium nations re-decide every ~55–70 ticks, tribes every ~40–80; each holds a 30–40%-of-max reserve and attacks with everything above it once troops pass 50–60% of max (vs neutral land the reserve gate is only 10–20%, so a rival with open border always keeps growing). The tell is a sharp `troops` drop since your last decision while `tiles` stayed flat. Hit within one decision of that drop, while its troops sit near reserve.
- Forced retaliation: if non-retreating own `incoming_attacks` total ≥20–25% of your troops, act now. If the attacker borders you, land-attack it with ≥ its incoming troops — a fresh attack on the exact attacker cancels its inbound troop-for-troop and the surplus is your push (a counter smaller than the inbound is absorbed reducing their attack, not creating a push). If it does not border you (boat), don't strip home: boat its source, or build a defense post on the landing front.
- Alliances (Medium target), in priority order: your `troops` > 2.5× its `troops` accepts even at Hostile (threat overrides relation); relation Friendly accepts; ~70% accept in the first 1800+spawn ticks; otherwise "similarly strong" (your troops >70–80% of its, or your tiles >80–90% of its with your troops >50%). It rejects below Neutral, once it holds ~4–5 alliances, and always right after you hit it (relation −70, pending request auto-rejected); spawn-phase requests auto-reject. Request only nations you won't fight; tribes accept every request — ally the ones you won't eat. A Medium/Hard ally betrays you once it reaches 10× your troops (or 3× when it borders only you) or you fall under 20% of your max, so an alliance is safest while you are the stronger side.
- Defense posts: a nation builds one when non-retreating land incoming (no `sourceTile`) reaches 35% of its `troops` — Medium: 50% chance per structure call, one post, 50k gold, 50-tick build, 3 calls per cadence. Inside its 30-tile range your per-tile loss is ×5 and capture speed ÷3. A boat landing is an attack with a `sourceTile`, so it never trips that trigger — land by sea against a defender that will post, or finish the land push inside 50 ticks, or pivot.
- Dogpile: a bordering nation/tribe whose `incoming_troops` ≥50% of its `troops` (discount your own fresh push) is being drained by others; join cheaply — it can't spare a counter. If your own incoming total reaches ~50% of your troops you are the dogpile: stop advancing, counter the largest attacker, build a post.

## Attack sizing (derive every size; never a fixed share)
- Neutral land: flat 16 troops/tile plains, 20 highland, 24 mountain; extra troops buy only capture speed and leftovers return when the front closes. Send ≈ (tiles you mean to take) × terrain cost.
- Players: per-tile loss = 0.48·mag·clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2) + 0.0052·mag·(D/T), where D = target `troops`, T = target `tiles`, mag = 80/100/120 on plains/highland/mountain, ×0.7 vs a tribe, ×5 inside a post. Sending A ≥ 1.667·D pins the clamp at its 0.6 floor (cheapest and fastest per tile); below ~0.7·D you bleed 2×+; far above is waste. Erasing T tiles ≈ 0.288·mag·T + 0.0052·mag·D (plains, no post).
- Worked tribe (D=5,000, T=2,000, density 2.5, plains): per tile ≈ 0.7·(0.288·80 + 0.0052·80·2.5) ≈ 17; open with ≈8,500; all 2,000 tiles ≈34k.
- Worked nation (D=100,000, T=10,000, density 10, plains): per tile ≈ 23.04 + 0.416·10 ≈ 27; open with ≥167k; erasing all 10,000 tiles ≈272k.
- Worked neutral: 1,000 tiles ≈16k plains, 20k highland, 24k mountain.
- Peel low-density targets (many tiles, few troops) first; mountains last. Size a boat landing like a land push (≥1.667·D) — a token force only feeds the defender. Boats are free, cap 3, 1 tile/tick, aimed from `boat_targets` (≤30-tile straight water rays); launch early and far, and a landing that arrives after you already own the tile only refunds the troops minus 25%.
