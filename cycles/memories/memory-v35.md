# OpenFront solo playbook — land, tempo, survival

## Ethos
- You act once per 50 ticks (5 s). Every decision moves an attack, a boat order, or a build — never idle twice. Confirm it landed in the next `attacks`/`units`; if it never appears it was blocked (spawn immunity, no shared border, friendly/ally target) — re-aim, don't resend blindly or rebuild the same tile.
- Growth = (10 + troops^0.73/4)·(1 − troops/max), ×0.95 for Medium nations, ×0.5 for tribes; max = 2·(tiles^0.6·1000 + 50k) + 250k·city levels. Humans keep the full cap, Medium nations 0.75×, tribes 1/3, so equal land beats them. Attacking lowers troops toward the cap, which *raises* refill — so keep the army working, but only into targets you can finish. The first cities are the cheapest permanent cap (125k/+250k, then doubling).
- A run dies two ways: stalling against water or a defended front, and farming cheap land while a bordering nation refills unopposed. Fix both every few decisions — boats keep land opening; alliance or an early capped strike stops the neighbour that would roll you.

## When to act
- Refill cadence: Medium nations re-decide every 55–70 ticks, tribes every 40–80. A bordering rival whose `troops` fell sharply since the last decision while `tiles` stayed flat has just spent to its 30–40%-of-max reserve (it wars down to reserve but expands neutrals only to 10–20%): that is the counter window. Hit within one decision with ~1.7× its current `troops`, and refeed as it climbs. Never open while it sits near its observed peak and rising.
- Forced retaliation: if non-retreating `incoming_attacks` sum to ≥ ~20–25% of your troops, act at once. If the attacker `borders_human`, land-attack it with ≥ the incoming troops — a new attack cancels an inbound from the same player troop-for-troop at launch, so the surplus is your push. If it does not border you (boat), don't strip home: boat its source, or build a defense post on the landing front.
- Alliances: a Medium nation accepts when your `troops` > 2.5× its own (threat overrides a hostile relation), when the relation is Friendly, in the first ~190s (70% at ≥ Neutral), or when you hold ≥ ~70–80% of its troops (or more tiles than ~80–90% of its, with ≥ half its troops). It rejects below Neutral, once it holds ~4–6 alliances, and always after you attack it (−70 relation, embargo, pending request auto-rejected). Request only from nations you will not fight; tribes accept every request, so ally the ones you won't chew and eat the rest.
- Defense posts: a nation spawns one when incoming *land* attacks reach ≥35% of its `troops` (Medium 50% chance per call, 1 post at a time; Hard/Impossible ceil(ratio/0.4)), for 50k+ gold and a 50-tick build. Inside a post's 30-tile range your per-tile loss is ×5 and speed ×1/3. A boat landing never triggers a post — so against a defended nation press fronts the post misses, land by sea, or pivot the axis instead of grinding.
- Dogpile: a bordering nation or tribe with `incoming_troops` ≥ 50% of its `troops` is being drained by others (the engine's victim threshold) — join cheaply; it can't spare a counter. If your own incoming sum reaches ~50% of your troops you are the dogpile: stop advancing, cancel into the largest attacker, build a post.

## Attack sizing (derive every size, never a fixed share)
- Neutral land costs a flat 16 troops/tile on plains, 20 highland, 24 mountain (human attacker). Cost is independent of force size, so send ≈ tiles-you-mean-to-take × terrain cost; extra troops only buy capture speed and leftovers return when the front closes.
- Players: per-tile attacker loss ≈ 0.48·clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2)·mag + 0.0052·mag·(D/T), where D = target `troops`, T = target `tiles` (both readable), mag = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, ×0.7 vs a tribe, ×5 inside a post; very large (>150k-tile) defenders cut the first term to 0.7×. Sending A ≥ 1.667·D pins the clamp at its 0.6 floor — cheapest and fastest per tile; below ~0.7·D you bleed 2×+, far above wastes troops. The defender loses D/T troops for every tile you take.
- Erasing a target costs ≈ 0.288·mag·T + 0.0052·mag·D attacker troops (plains, no post, small defender) ≈ 2.7·D at density 10. Commit only when you can refeed and finish; a half-done target refills.
- Worked tribe: D=5,000, T=2,000 (density 2.5), plains, ×0.7 mag → ~16.9/tile; A ≈ 8,500 opens it, all 2,000 tiles cost ~34k. Worked nation: D=100,000, T=10,000 (density 10), plains → ~27.2/tile; A ≈ 170k opens, all 10k tiles cost ~272k. Worked neutral: 1,000 plains tiles ≈ 16k, highland 20k, mountain 24k. Peel low-density (many tiles, few troops) targets first; terrain self-sorts to plains, so posts and mountains only slow you.
- Transport ships: cap 3, 1 tile/tick (10 tiles/s), free; pick land from `boat_targets` (≤30-tile straight water rays) and launch early and far. A landing rides the same per-tile math and ignores the post trigger; if the destination is still yours it just returns the troops (−25%). Size a landing like a land push (~1.7×D) — a token force only feeds the defender.
