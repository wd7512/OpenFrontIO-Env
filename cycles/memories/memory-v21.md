# Playbook — solo FFA vs nations + tribes

## Ethos
- One order per 50-tick decision (5 s). Spend it on the single best action: survive, finish a target, take cheap neutral land, or build. Idle and half-measures both lose; a dead player takes no land.
- Survival gates everything: you are one actor against ~50 nations acting every 55-70 ticks and 400 tribes every 40-80. Keep home troops >= the strongest non-allied bordering rival's troops — a Medium AI's `weakest` pick attacks only when it has more troops than you, and its `hated` pick attacks up to 3x yours.
- One war at a time; same-target land attacks merge, so concentrate force. Splitting across two targets means neither dies and home is naked.
- Finish or skip: a player under 100 tiles is auto-conquered, its land split among bordering attackers. Never grind a defender that regrows faster than you kill it — but canceling a land attack on a player (or recalling a boat) burns 25% of its troops, so only commit what you can finish.
- Land drives maxTroops = 2*(tiles^0.6*1000 + 50000) + 250k per city level (you x1, Medium nations x0.75, tribes x1/3), so neutral border is permanent growth worth taking every safe decision. Cities are your reserve stat; gold is 100/tick and cities cost 125k/250k/500k on their own counter (ports/factories share a separate one), so budget ~2-3 a run, convert surplus gold to cities when safe, and never order what you cannot pay.

## When to act
- RIVAL REFILL: a Medium nation acts every 55-70 ticks, a tribe every 40-80. It acts only above reserve (30-40% of its max) and reliably only above trigger (50-60%); below trigger it tries ~10% of cycles. On acting it sends everything above reserve at one target (neighboring bots first), merging same-target attacks.
- COUNTER WINDOW: if a rival's troops drop >= ~15% over one decision while its tiles hold steady, it just spent and now sits at reserve, refilling over ~100+ ticks. Strike immediately — that is the cheap window.
- FORCED RETALIATION: on its act tick a Medium nation answers its largest non-bot incoming attacker with everything above reserve — no too-weak filter and no stronger-neighbor cap. Head-to-head attacks cancel (larger deletes smaller, remainder advances). Never start a fight you cannot out-size; to erase an incoming X send > X, and expect a full counter if it still holds above reserve. On your side, if your incoming_troops sum >= 50% of your troops, stop expanding and send > the largest attacker (that also cancels their attack).
- ALLIANCES (Medium): accept if you are a threat (you hold > 2.5x its troops — threat is checked before relation), if relation is Friendly, ~70% before ~tick 1900, or if you are similarly strong (your troops > ~70-80% of its, or comparable tiles). It rejects below Neutral, at ~4-5 existing allies, and ~90% for traitors. Attacking auto-embargoes, drops relation -70 and auto-rejects your requests; breaking an alliance makes you a traitor ~300 ticks (all reject you; attackers lose only 0.5x). Request early, stay Friendly, never attack a wanted ally.
- DEFENSE POSTS: a defender that owns a structure builds one when the sum of LAND attacks (sourceTile null — boat landings do not count) reaches >= 35% of its current troops. Medium: 50% per structure call, max 1, calls ~every 20 ticks. Within 30 tiles a post makes attackers lose ~5x and capture ~3x slower, so a land push past 35% walls itself. Keep each probe under 35%, commit one decisive wave expecting 5x, or open a boat front (an existing post still applies 5x at the landing). A stalled front usually means a post went up.
- DOGFILE: target a rival whose incoming_troops >= 50% of its troops (and whose troops are <= ~1.2x yours) — it is already committed and low on reserve, so it counters weakly; prefer it to an idle equal-sized neighbor.
- BOATS: max 3, free, ~1 tile/tick, sinkable by warships. Use the listed boat_targets (straight water ray, first landfall, with owner/troops/tiles) when the land border is water or to bypass a post. Order early — they take many decisions to arrive — but not while home is exposed.
- Only hit bordering, non-immune players (borders_human true; attacks without a shared border retreat silently). Terrain is not exposed: price plains, and if a front underperforms, re-price as highland/mountain or a post.

## Attack sizing (derive from D, T, density, terrain — never a fixed share)
- Per-tile attacker loss vs a nation: L = 0.48*clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2)*m + 0.52*(D/T)*(m/100), where D = target troops, A = sent, T = target tiles, m = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain. Vs a tribe multiply L by 0.7 (m 56/70/84). D/A=1 is the knee; at A=D/2 loss hits its 2x cap. Beyond ~1.7D extra troops only speed capture, so cap A at ownTroops - strongestNeighborTroops (and keep reserve >= that rival's troops).
- To take all T tiles you must absorb ~T*L while the defender regrows (10 + troops^0.73/4)*(1 - troops/max) per tick (~50-80k/decision at 100-200k troops). Win only if A out-totals D + regen: efficient band A ~1.3-1.7D, never below ~1.0D. Survivors return when the front closes; a retreat burns 25%.
- Neutral land: L is flat 16/20/24 per tile whatever you send, and capture speed maxes from A ~6600. Land gained ~ A/L, so size the expand order near L * frontier tiles, not your whole army.
- Worked examples. Tribe 800 tiles / 50k (D/T=62, m=56): L ~ 0.48*0.6*56 + 0.52*62*0.56 ~ 34/tile, full clear ~27k; a 75-85k push (>= 1.5D) one-passes it, a 15k probe pays ~2x per tile and dies. Nation 3000 tiles / 200k on plains, no post (D/T=67): L ~ 23 + 28 = 51/tile, clear ~150k, but it regrows ~80k/decision, so send >= its troops + regen and finish in 1-2 decisions; with a post L ~ 5x the first term and capture 3x slower, so boat it or skip. Neutral 1000 plains tiles: ~16k troops total, send ~16k spread across the frontier.
