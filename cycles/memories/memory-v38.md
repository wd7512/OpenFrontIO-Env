# OpenFront solo playbook — tempo, boats, exchange rates

## Ethos
- One decision = 50 ticks (5 s). Make the single highest-value order each decision; idle decisions are lost growth. A fresh land attack on a target you already attack merges into that push, so re-attack to top a winner up — but cancel a losing push (cancel_attack / cancel_boat exist; ~25% retreat malus) instead of letting it drain to zero.
- Every player you touch takes a −70 relation hit (Medium) and auto-rejects any pending alliance from you — an attack is a permanent front. Few, water-bounded fronts beat a wide blob; the biggest empire becomes the map's dogpile. Prefer weak, low-density targets, then finish them.
- Hold ~30% of max in reserve for counters and posts; convert troops to tiles only when the front is quiet. Growth/tick = (10 + troops^0.73/4)·(1 − troops/max); max = 2·(tiles^0.6·1000 + 50000) + 250000·city levels. Human keeps 100% max/growth; Medium nations 75%/95%; tribes 1/3 and 50%. Cities (including captured) are how max scales; each next city costs 125k·2^n, so spend gold down steadily — a hoard is a stalled tempo.
- A land front inside 30 tiles of a defense post costs ×5 loss and ~3× slower capture. Boats never trigger posts: land by sea, or cut around a posted front — never grind into one.

## When to act
- Rival cadence: Medium nations re-decide every 55–70 ticks, tribes 40–80. Nations hold 30–40% of max and, once troops reach 50–60% of max, attack with everything above that reserve; into neutral they spend freely down to a 10–20% reserve every cadence, so race them for empty land. Tell of a spent rival: troops dropped sharply since your last read while tiles barely moved. Hit within one decision.
- Counter window: estimate a nation's max from its tiles (Medium ≈ 0.75·2·(T^0.6·1000 + 50000); a floor, cities add more). Troops below ~0.4× that estimate means it sits at reserve. Attack with strictly more than its current troops D (>1.667·D pins the cheap loss floor); ≤D is absorbed troop-for-troop. Refill is ~0.08×max per decision, so the window is 1–2 decisions. Skip targets flagged immune.
- Forced retaliation: if non-retreating own incoming_attacks total I ≥ ~25% of your troops, act now. If the largest attacker borders you, send A ≥ I + 1.667·(its current troops) — init cancels inbound 1:1 and the surplus pushes. If it does not border you (boat attacker), do not strip home: boat its coast, or post the landing front.
- Alliances (Medium), in order: accept if your troops > 2.5× theirs — the threat check runs before the relation check, so it overrides even Hostile; accept if relation is Friendly; ~70% accept before tick ~1900; else accept only similar strength (their troops > 0.7–0.8× yours, or their tiles > 0.8–0.9× yours with troops > 0.5× yours). Reject traitors, anyone already holding ~4–5 alliances, or relation below Neutral. Tribes accept every request — ally them freely. Never request from a player you are attacking: the engine rejects it on the spot. A Medium ally betrays once its troops ≥10× yours, or ≥3× if it borders only you — never let one ally dwarf you.
- Defense posts: a non-Easy nation builds one (50k, 50-tick build) once non-retreating land incoming (sourceTile null) ≥35% of its troops, 50% chance per structure call, ~3 calls per cadence. Boat attacks keep sourceTile non-null for their whole life, so they never trip it: land by sea, or finish a land push inside ~50 ticks / while the target holds <50k gold. Build your own post when your non-retreating land incoming ≥35% of troops.
- Dogpile: a bordering rival with incoming_troops ≥50% of its troops (discount your own fresh push) is a victim — join cheaply, it cannot spare a counter. If your own incoming reaches ~50% of troops you are the dogpile: stop advancing, counter the largest attacker, post, and shed a border.

## Attack sizing (derive every slider from D=troops, T=tiles, density D/T; never a fixed percent)
- Slider: percent = round(100·A / your current troops), clamped 1–100. Spend cap A ≤ troops − 0.3·M (M = your max), preserving the reserve; if A < 1, hold. Never send 100% while another front is live.
- Neutral land: 16/20/24 troops per tile plains/highland/mountain (terrain is invisible — budget 20–24 when unsure). Full speed needs A ≥ 400·speed (6,600 plains / 8,000 highland / 10,000 mountain); extra only buys speed. Keep an expand order running while a neutral border exists.
- Player target, per-tile loss once A ≥ 1.667·D (pins clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2) at its floor): 0.288·mag_eff + 0.0052·mag_eff·(D/T), with mag_eff = 80/100/120 plains/highland/mountain, ×0.7 vs a tribe, ×5 inside a post. Below ~0.7·D the loss runs toward 2×. Full erasure ≈ 0.288·mag_eff·T + 0.0052·mag_eff·D; open A ≥ 1.667·D plus a cushion, then top up the merged push. Attackers above 100k tiles also earn a loss bonus.
- Worked tribe (D=5,000, T=2,000, density 2.5, plains): per tile 16.13 + 0.291·2.5 ≈ 17; open A ≥ 1.667·5,000 ≈ 8,300; erase ≈ 16.13·2,000 + 0.291·5,000 ≈ 34k.
- Worked nation (D=100,000, T=10,000, density 10, plains, no post): per tile 23.04 + 0.416·10 ≈ 27; open A ≥ 167k; erase ≈ 23.04·10,000 + 0.416·100,000 ≈ 272k. Inside a post the same tile is ≈136 — never attack it.
- Peel low-density (small D/T, many tiles) targets first; mountains and posted fronts last.
- Boats: free, cap 3 in flight, 1 tile/tick, aimed from boat_targets (≤30-tile straight water rays). Size the landing ≥1.667·D of the beach owner or it only feeds them; keep a slot moving whenever a reachable, non-friendly target exists. A landing on your own tile refunds troops −25%.
