# OpenFront solo playbook — borders, timing, exchange rates

## Ethos
- One decision = 50 ticks (5 s). Issue one sized order every decision (attack, boat, build, post), then read the next snapshot: if nothing moved, re-aim instead of resending. If tiles fall two decisions running you are losing — switch to defense, don't keep grinding.
- Land is only worth taking if you can hold the border. Every new land border is a durable enemy: attacking a player costs −70 relation on Medium, so you cannot ally someone you hit. Prefer water-bounded flanks and few fronts over maximum tiles; a big sprawling blob becomes the map's dogpile and gets carved up.
- Convert troops to tiles only when the front is quiet; otherwise hold a reserve to feed counters and posts. Repeated attacks on one target merge into one push, so commit to finishing a target rather than poking many.
- Growth/tick = (10 + troops^0.73/4)·(1 − troops/max), max = 2·(tiles^0.6·1000 + 50000) + 250000·city levels. Human keeps 100% max and growth; Medium nations 75%/95%; tribes 1/3 and 50%. At equal tiles you out-max any nation, but only if you survive the refill: cities (each +250k max, including captured ones) and a held reserve are how you scale.
- Spend gold down on structures, ports and cities; a hoard at the end means you stalled. Never leave a threatened front without a post.

## When to act
- Rival cadence: Medium nations re-decide every 55–70 ticks, tribes every 40–80. Both keep 30–40% of max in reserve and attack with everything above it once troops reach 50–60% of max; vs terra nullius the reserve gate is only 10–20%. Tell: troops dropped sharply since your last snapshot while tiles barely moved. Hit within one decision of the drop, while troops sit near reserve; refill from reserve is fast (~0.09·max per decision for a 1M-max nation), so the window is one to two decisions.
- Counter window: estimate a nation's max from its tiles (Medium ≈ 0.75·2·(tiles^0.6·1000+50000) — a floor, cities add more). Troops below ~0.4× that estimate means it is at reserve: attack now with strictly more than its current troops (an attack ≤ its inbound is merely absorbed troop-for-troop; only the surplus pushes).
- Neutral timing: a nation with any open neutral border or nearby tribe attacks that first and returns, so it is not yet coming for you. Race it for the neutral; expect its attention once the neutral is gone.
- Forced retaliation: if non-retreating own incoming_attacks total ≥ ~25% of your troops, act now. If the largest attacker borders you, send a fresh land attack larger than its inbound on you — init cancels inbound 1:1 and the surplus pushes. If it does not border you (a boat attacker), don't strip home: boat its source, or build a post on the landing front.
- Alliances (Medium), in order: your troops > 2.5× theirs accepts even at Hostile (threat overrides relation); Friendly accepts; ~70% accept before ~tick 1900 (requests at tick ≤ ~101 auto-reject); otherwise similarly strong (their troops > 70–80% of yours, or their tiles > 80–90% of yours with their troops > 50% of yours). Rejected below Neutral, by traitors, or once they hold ~4–5 alliances. Tribes accept every request. A Medium ally betrays when its troops ≥10× yours, or ≥3× if it borders only you. Ally nations you won't fight; drop allies you can't outgrow.
- Defense posts: a nation builds one (50k, 50-tick build) when non-retreating land incoming (sourceTile null) ≥35% of its troops, ~50% chance per structure call, ~3 calls per cadence. Inside 30 tiles your per-tile loss is ×5 and capture ~3× slower. A boat landing keeps sourceTile non-null for its whole life, so it never trips the trigger — land by sea, finish the land push within 50 ticks, or hit when the target can't afford 50k. Build your own post at a threatened city once your land incoming reaches ~35% of troops.
- Dogpile: a bordering rival with incoming_troops ≥50% of its troops (discount your own fresh push) is a victim — join cheaply, it can't spare a counter. If your own incoming total reaches ~50% of troops you are the dogpile: stop advancing, counter the largest attacker, post, and shed borders.

## Attack sizing (derive each size from D=troops, T=tiles; never a fixed share)
- Neutral land: flat 16 troops/tile plains, 20 highland, 24 mountain (terrain is invisible, so budget 20–24 when unsure). Extra troops only buy speed: each tile consumes clamp(2000·speed/A, 5, 100) of the border budget, so A ≥ ~6,600 plains (≈8,000 highland, 10,000 mountain) reaches the 5 floor and full speed. 1,000 tiles ≈ 16k/20k/24k.
- Player target, per-tile attacker loss while A ≥ 1.667·D (this pins clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2) at its 0.6 floor, the cheapest and fastest rate): 0.288·mag_eff + 0.0052·mag_eff·(D/T), where mag_eff = 80/100/120 plains/highland/mountain, ×0.7 vs a tribe, ×5 inside a post. Below ~0.7·D the loss runs toward 2×. Erasing all T tiles ≈ 0.288·mag_eff·T + 0.0052·mag_eff·D; open with ≥1.667·D plus a cushion so A stays pinned as it drains.
- Worked tribe (D=5,000, T=2,000, density 2.5, plains): per tile 16.13 + 0.291·2.5 ≈ 17; open ≥1.667·5,000 ≈ 8,300 (about 10–12k to chew through); fully erase ≈16.13·2,000 + 0.291·5,000 ≈ 34k.
- Worked nation (D=100,000, T=10,000, density 10, plains, no post): per tile 23.04 + 0.416·10 ≈ 27; open ≥167k; fully erase ≈23.04·10,000 + 0.416·100,000 ≈ 272k. Inside a post the same tile is 5× (≈115 + 2.08·10 ≈ 136) — never land-attack a post.
- Peel low-density (small D/T, many tiles) targets first; mountains and posted fronts last.
- Boats: free, cap 3, 1 tile/tick, aimed from boat_targets (≤30-tile straight water rays). Size the landing ≥1.667·D of the beach owner or it only feeds them. Keep a slot moving whenever a target is reachable. A landing that arrives on your own tile refunds the troops minus 25%; boats are the tool against any defender that would post.
