# Playbook — solo FFA vs nations + tribes

## Ethos
- One order-set per 50-tick decision. Every decision must take land, finish a target, counter an incoming, or build. Idle decisions lose the compounding race.
- Land is the growth stat: maxTroops = 2*(tiles^0.6*1000 + 50000) + 250000 per city level (Medium nation x0.75, Hard x1, bot x1/3). Expand every decision a neutral border exists — early land is cheap and permanent, and nations claim it if you wait.
- One war at a time. Same-target land attacks merge into one; splitting force across two targets means neither finishes and home is naked.
- Finish or skip: a player driven under 100 tiles is auto-conquered and its land redistributes to bordering attackers. Never grind a defender that regrows faster than you kill it.
- Keep home reserve >= the strongest bordering rival's troops. A nation cannot act while its own troops sit below reserve (30-40% of its max), so pushing a rival under its reserve also disarms it. Cap every attack at ownTroops - that rival's troops.
- Growth is income: gold accrues 100/tick; a city adds 250k max (cost 2^n*125k, double each time: 125k, 250k, 500k, 1M cap), port/factory share that counter and give trade gold. Check gold before ordering a build — an unpayable order wastes the decision.

## When to act
- Rival cadence: a Medium nation acts every 55-70 ticks; it can attack only while troops >= reserve (30-40% of its max) and reliably only above trigger (50-60% of max) — below trigger it tries on just 10% of its act ticks. On acting it sends everything above reserve at one target, then sits at reserve and needs ~100+ ticks to refill. A sharp troop drop (>=~15% since your last decision) with tiles steady = it just spent: that is the counter window; strike before it refills.
- Forced retaliation: on its act tick a Medium nation answers its largest non-bot incoming attacker with everything above reserve — no too-weak filter, no neighbor cap. Attacks between two players cancel head-to-head (larger deletes smaller, remainder advances). Never open a fight you cannot out-size in the same exchange: to erase an incoming X send > X, and expect a full counter if it still holds above reserve.
- Alliances (Medium): accepted when you hold >2.5x its troops (threat is checked before relation), when relation is Friendly, ~70% before ~tick 1900, or when you are similarly strong (>70-80% of its troops or comparable tiles); rejected below Neutral, at 4-5 existing allies, ~90% for traitors. Attacking auto-embargoes and auto-rejects (relation -70), so never attack someone you still want as an ally; breaking an alliance marks you traitor ~300 ticks (rejected everywhere, and attacks on you cost the enemy x0.5). Request early, stay Friendly, request a bordering rival not far nations.
- Defense posts: a defender spawns one only from a LAND attack (sourceTile null) whose total reaches >=35% of its current troops, once it owns a structure; Medium builds on 50% of structure calls, max 1, and calls fire roughly every ~20 ticks. Within 30 tiles of a post attacker loss x5 and capture x3 slower, so a land push past 35% walls itself, and a stalled front usually means a post went up. Keep a probe's sum under 35%, commit a decisive wave expecting x5, or open a new front by boat. Transport landings (sourceTile set) never trigger a post, but an existing post still applies x5 at the landing.
- Dogpile: a bordering rival whose incoming_troops minus your own attack on it is >=50% of its troops is already committed — prefer it to an idle neighbor of equal size. If your own incoming sum reaches >=50% of your troops, stop expanding and counter the largest attacker.
- Boats: max 3, ~1 tile/tick, sinkable by warships. Use listed boat_targets (straight water rays, first landfall, with owner/troops/tiles) for islands and post-free beaches land can't reach; they take several decisions to arrive, so order them early when expansion stalls against water.

## Attack sizing (derive from D, T, density; never a fixed share)
- Per-tile attacker loss vs a player (no post): L = 0.48*clamp(D/A,0.6,2)*m + 0.52*(D/T)*(m/100), where D = target troops, A = sent, T = target tiles, m = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, x0.7 vs a tribe (56/70/84). D/A=1 is the knee; below A=D the first term doubles. A defender post in range multiplies the first term x5 and slows capture x3. Terrain is not shown, so price on plains and treat a front that underperforms as highland/mountain (+25-50% cost, slower).
- To clear all T tiles you must absorb ~T*L, and the defender regrows (10 + troops^0.73/4)*(1 - troops/max) per tick. Win only if A can out-total D + regen over the fight; efficient band A ~1.3-1.7*D, never below ~1.0*D. Leftover attack troops refund when the front closes.
- Neutral land: L is flat m/5 = 16/20/24 per tile whatever you send, and capture speed maxes from A ~6600. Land gained ~ A/16, so size A ~16*frontierTiles and keep one expand order live.
- Worked examples. Tribe, 800 tiles / 50k (d=62, m=56): L ~ 0.48*0.6*56 + 0.52*62*0.56 = 34/tile, full clear ~27k; a 70-85k push (>=1.67D) kills it in one pass, while a 15k probe pays 72/tile and dies. Nation, 3000 tiles / 200k (d=67, plains, no post): A ~330k gives L ~ 23 + 28 = 51/tile, clear ~150k, but it regrows ~70k/decision — send >= its troops + regen and finish in 1-2 decisions; in post range L ~140/tile and 3x slower, so boat it or skip. Neutral: 1000 plains tiles ~ 16k troops total; A ~16*frontier at any strength.
