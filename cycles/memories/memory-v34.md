# OpenFront solo playbook — every decision must buy land

## Ethos
- Land share wins. Each decision should move an attack, a boat or a build; never idle two decisions in a row. Confirm every order in the next overview (`attacks`/`units`); an order that never appears was blocked (spawn immunity, no shared border, friendly target) — re-aim, don't resend blindly or build the same tile twice.
- Growth = (10 + troops^0.73/4)·(1 − troops/max). Attacking keeps you under the cap, so it *raises* refill; sitting full wastes income. max = 2·(tiles^0.6·1000 + 50k) + 250k per city level — cities are the cheapest permanent cap. Humans keep the full cap, Medium nations only 0.75×, so equal land beats them.
- You act once per 50 ticks (5 s). Read the fresh overview first. Tempo beats safety: a run is lost by stalling late, not by an aggressive early move.

## When to act
- Refill cadence: Medium nations tick every 55–70, tribes every 40–80. Nations attack at 50–60% of max and spend down to a 30–40% reserve (expansions only 10–20%). A bordering rival whose `troops` fell sharply since the last decision while `tiles` stayed flat has just spent: that is the counter window — hit it within one decision with ~1.7–2.2× its current troops. Don't open a big fight while it sits near its observed peak and climbing.
- Forced retaliation: if your `incoming_attacks` sum to ≥ ~20–25% of your troops, act at once. If the attacker `borders_human`, order a land attack on it of ≥ that attack's troops — a new attack and an inbound from the same player cancel troop-for-troop at launch, so the surplus is your push. If it does not border you (boat), don't strip home: boat its source and/or post a defense post on the landing front.
- Alliances: a nation accepts when your troops > 2.5× its own (threat overrides a hostile relation), when relation is Friendly, in the opening ~3 min post-spawn (Medium ~70% at relation ≥ Neutral), or when you are comparably strong (within ~70–90% of its troops/tiles). It rejects below Neutral, once it holds ~4–6 alliances, and (Hard+) when it already borders 2+ non-allies. Request only from nations you will not attack: attacking costs −70 relation, an embargo, and auto-rejects its pending request. Tribes accept every request.
- Defense posts appear only against land attacks (a boat landing carries a source tile and never triggers one) totalling ≥35% of the defender's troops: Medium 50% chance for one post, Hard/Impossible ceil(ratio/0.4) posts, 50-tick build. Inside a post's 30-tile range your per-tile cost is ×5 and it shells boats within 75, so press fronts it doesn't cover and land by sea; when a post appears, pivot the axis rather than grind it.
- Dogpile: a bordering nation or tribe with `incoming_troops` ≥ ~50% of its troops is being drained by others — join cheaply; it can't spare a counter and loses D/T per tile. If your own incoming_troops reach ~50% of your troops you are the dogpile: stop advancing, cancel into the largest attacker, build a post.

## Attack sizing (derive, never a fixed share)
- Neutral land costs a flat 16 troops/tile on plains, 20 highland, 24 mountain, whatever the size. 500 tiles ≈ 8–12k. Extra troops only buy capture rate and leftovers return when the front closes, so send ~20–25× the tiles you mean to take, no more.
- Players: per-tile attacker loss = 0.6·clamp(D/A, 0.6, 2)·mag·0.8·debuff + 0.4·1.3·(D/T)·(mag/100), where D = target `troops`, T = target `tiles` (both readable), mag = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, ×0.7 against a tribe, ×5 inside a post, debuff 0.7–1.0 for very large defenders. The defender loses D/T troops for every tile you take.
- Size A ≈ 1.7–2.2×D: that pins the clamp at its 0.6 floor (cheapest and fastest per tile). Below ~0.6×D you bleed badly; far above it you only waste troops. Erasing a target costs about 0.29·mag·T + 0.42·D attackers (plains, no post) ≈ 2.7× its troops at density 10, so commit only when you can refeed and finish — a half-done target refills.
- Worked tribe: D=5,000, T=2,000 (density 2.5), plains, ×0.7 mag → ~17/tile; ~1.7×D ≈ 8,500 opens it and every 10k of push claims ~600 tiles. Worked nation: D=100,000, T=10,000 (density 10), plains → ~27/tile; 200k claims ~7,400 tiles and drains ~74k of their troops, then a ~70k refeed finishes. Worked neutral: 1,000 plains tiles ≈ 16k, highland 20k, mountain 24k.
- Terrain self-sorts to the cheapest tiles first, so post/mountain fronts only slow you; plains are best. Boats ride the same per-tile math but ignore the post trigger — use them against defended nations, islands, and whenever no neutral land borders you.
- Keep up to 3 transport ships sailing from `boat_targets`; a landing resolves into a free attack or seeds expansion on another landmass. Ships cross ~1 tile/tick, so launch early and far, and size a landing like a land push (~1.7×D) — a token force just feeds the defender.
