# Playbook — solo FFA vs nations + tribes (Medium)

## Ethos
- One order-set per 50-tick decision. Every decision must take land, finish a target, offset incoming, or build. Idling is how runs die: the sim keeps ticking and rivals keep compounding while you do nothing.
- Land is the growth stat (maxTroops ~ 2*(tiles^0.6*1000 + 50k) + 250k per city level). Tribes cap at a third of raw max and delete structures, so they are early food, not a plan. Nations compound on tiles+cities: kill the weakest, nearest ones before they outgrow you.
- One war at a time. Same-target land attacks merge, but spreading force over 3+ targets means none finishes and your reserve is gone.
- Finish or skip. Any player under 100 tiles is auto-conquered and its land redistributes to bordering attackers. Grinding a target back to full with small stacks wastes the run.
- Feed income and defense with the war: ports/factories make gold, a city costs 125k*2^n and needs income first, SAMs/posts keep you alive. Check your gold before ordering a build — an unpayable order burns the whole decision.

## When to act
- Rival cadence: a Medium nation re-decides every 55-70 ticks. On its act tick it sends down to 30-40% of max (reserve) and holds only 30-40%, then must regrow to the 50-60% trigger to act again (only 10% chance per tick below trigger). A sharp troop drop since your last look = it just spent: that is the counter window. Strike the drop, not the fat 50-60% state.
- Forced retaliation: on its act tick a Medium nation answers the largest incoming non-bot attacker with everything above reserve; Medium has no "too-weak" filter and no neighbor cap. Attacks between two players cancel head-to-head — the larger attack deletes the smaller and its remainder advances. Never open a nation you cannot out-size in the same exchange: to erase an incoming attack of X, send > X, or do not open.
- Alliances (Medium): accepted when you hold >2.5x its troops (threat is checked before relation), when relation is Friendly, or ~70% before ~tick 1900 (spawn+1800). Rejected below Neutral, at 4-5 existing allies, ~90% for traitors, and by anyone you attacked (attacking auto-embargoes it and auto-rejects its pending request, relation ~-70). Request early, stay Friendly, and never attack someone you still want as an ally.
- Defense posts: a defender builds one only from a LAND attack (sourceTile null) summing to >=35% of its own troops, and only after it already owns a structure; Medium rolls 50% per structure call, max 1. Keep a land raid under 35% of its troops, or hit a front >30 tiles from any post. A post within 30 of a tile makes it cost x5 attacker loss and x3 slower — the wall a surprise push dies on.
- Boats avoid posts: a transport landing (sourceTile set) never triggers post creation, so boats open water-blocked fronts and post-free beaches; an existing post still applies x5 within 30 of the landing/target. Boat targets come from shore rays, take several decisions to arrive, and enemy warships can sink them.
- Dogpile: a bordering rival whose incoming_troops >= 50% of its own troops (subtract your own attack on it first) is a victim whose army is already committed — prefer it to an idle neighbor of the same size. If your own incoming sum reaches ~50% of your troops, stop expanding and offset/consolidate.
- Betrayal: breaking an alliance marks you a traitor ~300 ticks — nations refuse/reject you and attacks on you are cheaper. Only break to finish the target in the same push.

## Attack sizing (derive every number from D, T, D/T, terrain; never a fixed share)
- Per-tile attacker loss vs a player (sourceTile null): L = 0.48*clamp(D/A,0.6,2)*m + 0.52*(D/T)*(m/100), with m = 80 plains / 100 highland / 120 mountain, x0.7 vs a tribe, x5 if a defender post is in range. D = target troops, A = sent, T = target tiles, D/T = density. L stops falling at A = 1.67*D; capture rate saturates near A = D. Efficient band is A ~ 1.3-1.7*D. The defender also loses D/T troops per tile, so a long sweep thins it. Cap A <= ownTroops - strongest bordering rival's troops.
- Tribe (m=56), 800 tiles / 50k troops (density 62): A ~1.67*D ~84k gives L ~34/tile, sweep ~25-30k. One push of ~70-85k kills it; a 9-20k dribble dies at ~200 tiles and it regrows.
- Nation (plains, no post), 3000 tiles / 200k troops (density 67): A ~330k gives L ~50/tile, sweep ~150k + its regen. In post range L ~250/tile and 3x slower. If A_req > ownTroops - reserve, do not open: boat it, take a post-free axis, or wait.
- Neutral land: L is flat m/5 = 16/20/24 per tile whatever you send, and capture rate is maxed from ~6600 troops (below that each tile consumes more of the tile budget). Total land ~ A/16, so size A ~ 16*frontierTiles (200 plains tiles ~4-6k) and push several frontiers in parallel; survivors refund when a frontier closes.
- Terrain: highland/mountain cost 25-50% more per tile and move slower — prefer plains. Confirm every order can land (shared border or a listed boat tile) and can be paid for; a rejected order wastes the decision.
